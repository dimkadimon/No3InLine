It is attached to the distributed reader PDF as \path{no3-replay-r2.zip} and also supplied in the manuscript source archive under \path{anc/}. \paragraph{Where the points are.} In \path{results/lower-bounds.csv}, the last column gives the exact coordinates or certificate for each of the 15 sides; the preceding columns identify the comparison and its transfer. A CSV with header \texttt{x,y} is itself a zero-based coordinate certificate. The small anchored examples have both CSVs and compact certificates in \path{small-prime-examples/}. The large matching uses \path{unbounded-gain/data/certificate.txt}. Its header gives $p,k$, the number of opposite pairs and the number of flipped bits, followed by the representatives and the least residue of each flipped component. The base orientation has even physical parity at square abscissas. For the large two-period frames, the files named \texttt{final-} or \texttt{edits\_} give the prime, deletion and addition counts, then the actual edits. The kit's \path{README.md} specifies the frame and gives example expansion commands.

\paragraph{Checks that do not need the network.} Unzip into an empty directory. From its root use Python 3.9 or newer without \verb|-O|, and a C++17 compiler. No search solver is needed. Run
\begin{verbatim}
python3 scripts/verify_release.py --hashes-only
python3 scripts/comparison.py
python3 scripts/verify_release.py
\end{verbatim}
The first checks file integrity, the second checks transfers and both panels of Table~\ref{tab:bounds}, and the third reconstructs and checks all 15 project witnesses. It regenerates the large matching byte-for-byte before running a separately implemented integer verifier on the resulting certificate. To export its full coordinates use \path{scripts/expand_ring.py} with arguments \path{unbounded-gain/data/certificate.txt} and a new CSV filename. The sorted export has $4\,782\,972$ data rows. Its digest and the six smaller ring comparisons are in \path{verification/exported-rings.txt}. The finite counting diagnostics in \path{scripts/audit_counting.py} can also be run on their own.

\paragraph{Rechecking an external comparison.} \path{results/source-register.csv} gives the versioned manuscript URLs, credited authors, dates and comment locators. The exact two supplement ZIP hashes and download URLs are pinned in \path{scripts/restore_inputs.py} and \path{THIRD_PARTY.md}. To check K and S from those inputs run
\begin{verbatim}
python3 scripts/restore_inputs.py --group station --fetch
python3 scripts/comparison.py --audit-sources
python3 scripts/check_public_frames.py
\end{verbatim}
The first step refuses changed bytes; \verb|--cache DIR| works offline with the pinned archives. The second tests all 146 K sets and every relevant crop. The third reconstructs and checks the three S baselines without project exchanges. For F there are only the reports and their arithmetic: the exact locations and counts in \path{results/forum-counts.csv} do not constitute coordinate certificates.
