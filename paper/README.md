# Manuscript · R4 working preprint

**Dmitry Kamenetsky, *A super-polylogarithmic excess in the no-three-in-line problem*.** Illustrated working manuscript. [ArXiv submission sheet](arxiv-submission.md) · [Paste-ready abstract](arxiv-abstract.txt). [Response to the latest review](../REVIEW-RESPONSE.md) · [Earlier R3 response](../REVISION.md) · [Diagrams and their data](FIGURES.md).

**Start on pages 1–2.** The picture on side 293 shows the ring and its eight anchors, one blocked line, and why the flip keeps 440 points while the insertions raise that count to 442 and then 450. The general case is introduced by counting ring points, anchors and opposite pairs separately. The formal construction starts in §2. For the denominator **114** in Theorem 1.1, §8 derives a lower bound on the index directly from Stewart’s inequality with constant 104.

- **`main.pdf`** is the reader edition. The abstract ends with a live link to the public repository. The mathematical references and separately labelled construction/data credits precede Appendix A. A copy of the immutable `no3-replay-r2.zip` is attached; save it from the attachments pane, use `pdfdetach -save 1 -o replay.zip main.pdf`, or use the identical [commit-pinned download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dimkadimon/No3InLine/e19b8a1b689eba392c7d26ec3733e88db566dc0e/paper/anc/no3-replay-r2.zip).
- **`arxiv-source.tar.gz`** has 16 members: nine TeX inputs with one root (`main.tex`), four prebuilt vector figures, and three ancillary files (`anc/README.txt`, `anc/check-scales.py`, `anc/no3-replay-r2.zip`). TeX **does not read or execute `anc/`**. A TeX-only processor produces the same pages without the optional PDF attachment. See [arXiv ancillary-file guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/ancillary_files.html). No arXiv submission is implied.
- **Proof.** `proof.tex`, `collisions.tex` and `constants.tex` contain the analytic argument. Section 7.1 gives one explicit, intentionally very large sufficient range under the index condition; §8 distinguishes the direct test on the prime and index from Stewart's effective but unevaluated threshold on `j`. The result is about additive growth on an infinite sequence, not a larger leading coefficient.
- **Finite evidence.** `examples.tex` is the sole appendix. It includes the finite scale of Proposition 7.2 and the first cyclotomic input. Table 1 uses analytic or coordinate-backed comparisons. Table 2, with its own warning, gives the four unverified forum transfers, including the two negative differences. [`scripts/review_tables.py`](../scripts/review_tables.py) derives both tiers from the same data used in the unchanged replay kit; the old `table-ring.tex` is a historical output, not a typesetting input. `figures/` contains the prebuilt artwork. Only redrawing needs Matplotlib.

**Build or check.** From a fresh unpack of the source tarball, run `pdflatex -no-shell-escape main.tex` three times. Use standard AMS, Latin Modern, microtype, geometry, booktabs, tabularx, graphicx, xcolor and hyperref. No network or BibTeX is needed. Run `python3 anc/check-scales.py` separately for the matrix count, certified cyclotomic factorization, orders and finite comparison scales. From the full checkout `python3 scripts/build_paper.py` independently unpacks the archive, typesets it and checks the reader's text, links, both tables, and attachment. Byte-identical deliverables are expected on the recorded toolchain, not on arbitrary TeX/font versions.
