## Earlier response: review of R2

The earlier report assessed the 18-page R2 reader. The response used the later illustrated manuscript, keeping its repository link and finite replay ZIP. The review revision retained the original statement of Theorem 1.1. The current draft sharpens its exponent denominator to **114** in §8; the earlier counting estimates and all 15 project witness counts are unchanged. The newly quantified estimates below, like the rest of the asymptotic argument, are **working mathematics awaiting specialist review**.

### What improves, if the proof holds?

The abstract and introduction now call the guarantee a **super-polylogarithmic excess** and define it. On one infinite sequence the excess over `3n/2` dominates every fixed power of `log n`. If confirmed, this would be, to our knowledge, the first proof of *unbounded additive excess* over `3n/2` on an infinite sequence of square grids, more than fifty years after Hall–Jackson–Sudbery–Wild. It neither raises the leading coefficient nor supplies the improvement at every sufficiently large side. Finite records, the previous anchored `+1/2`, and partial-direction constructions are different claims. Half-turn invariance is an extra property of the constructed sets, not a restriction needed in order to obtain the unrestricted corollary. There is no claim of an exhaustive priority search.
