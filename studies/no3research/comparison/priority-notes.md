# Comparison basis — checked 19 November 2026, Brisbane time

**The baseline for the main result is the `full` column of the *minimal-defect* table**, not the columns of the better-known `2n` catalogue. The reference file `flammenkamp-2026-05-03.txt` is Achim Flammenkamp's dated public table, from https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/no3in/table_min_defect (SHA-256 `057a2ae75457e01fcb8464bbc0d3f317078742463baaf88191edb70b7719b55c`). The fixed-width spacing is significant. At 35, 37 and 39 the entry is `6` with no count; the later odd rows have blank `full` entries. The even rows contain values well beyond 39. The high cutoffs mentioned for **zero-defect** symmetry classes in https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/no3in/table.html do not count all suboptimal packings.

**Related ideas that precede this draft.** Flammenkamp credits John Selfridge for nearest missed symmetry and explicitly lists the order-three example: https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/no3in/symmetry_remarks.html . The miss there is measured using an isometry. Our deletion quantity uses the intersection over all eight isometries. We do not claim to have originated near-symmetry, symmetry-reduced search or orbit methods. See also Flammenkamp (1992, 1998) and Prellberg, arXiv:2602.07751 / doi:10.1016/j.jcta.2026.106244. Mitchell Riley's order-47 uniqueness in the record log concerns a **94**-point set with both diagonal symmetries. The **92**-point unique maximizer in this draft instead has all eight symmetries.

The public project https://github.com/aujurd22/no3inline-rigidity focuses on quarter-turn structure and full-size configurations, among other topics. Its `analysis/d4_reconstruction_results.txt` reconstructs oriented counts from 2n-point catalogue symmetry classes; `d4_relaxed.cpp` targets 2n points; `analysis/swarm_D4_scan.py` and `analysis/swarm_D4_seeddefects.py` concern changes to structured rot4 seeds. These particular files do not report our odd-board full-D4 packing census. Prellberg, arXiv:2605.09215, treats the **checkerboard-restricted** problem, including a symmetrized LP, rather than maximizing a fully symmetric packing on the entire board. Kudriashov, arXiv:2609.25133, includes a direction-spectrum analysis of ordinary extremal configurations and separate cube computations. These checks are not an exhaustive proof of priority.

**Unrestricted catalogue.** The comparison file is https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/no3in/download/all_known_solutions ; the October 28 update is described in https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/no3in/readme.html . SHA-256 `2aa1d0565e6792177c4dbb7a145257739d2008f7b9c126be7ea6be076f16eed5`, 431,017 words, 23,835,520 bytes. The archive itself is not redistributed here. The 13 new-to-snapshot words are ordinary 2n-point solutions, not 13 new board sizes. We searched every prefix and every dihedral image, not just the declared symmetry class. The 74-, 76- and 78-board inputs in `extras/frontier-seeds.txt` belong to this archive; they are not our discoveries. The five input words are every order-74/76/78 row of this byte snapshot.
