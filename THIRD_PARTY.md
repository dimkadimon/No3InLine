It is **not** a grant of rights in other authors' papers, software, underlying configurations, database collections, or embedded font programs. ## Mathematical inputs

- R. R. Hall, T. H. Jackson, A. Sudbery and K. Wild, [Some advances in the no-three-in-line problem](https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(75)90043-6), Theorem 2 (1975): the two-period classical benchmark. B. Kovács, Z. L. Nagy and D. R. Szabó, [Adv. Comb. 2026:7](https://doi.org/10.19086/aic.2026.7), Theorem 1.2, provides modern context; the case of at most two collinear points must be distinguished from larger limits.
- conic_lift_synthesist, [rStation 35](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/35), §5.4: the empirical six-site masks. cedar_conics and lattice_lift_research, [rStation 38](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/38): four-period ring, component orientations and Boolean clauses. [rStation 45](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/45): rooted secant identity. See also [31](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/31) on secant coverage and [33](https://rstation.ai/problems/no_three_in_line/papers/33) on pair exclusion. These manuscripts are cited as such, not presented as this project's discoveries.
- C. L. Stewart, [Acta Math. 211 (2013), 291–314](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11511-013-0105-y), Theorem 1, (7) in [arXiv:1008.1274](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.1274): the large prime divisor input. R. Lidl and H. Niederreiter, *Finite Fields*, 2nd ed., Theorem 5.41: the multiplicative character estimate.

In particular the reported 512 at side 288 makes a 504-point crop an incomplete ordinary benchmark at side 293.

For offline verification of the actual sets exhibited in the paper: **no upstream download is needed**. To reproduce historical package manifests and independently recheck the public comparison data, run `python3 scripts/restore_inputs.py --fetch`. If an upstream snapshot changes, the script stops at the wrong checksum; supply the dated files yourself via `--cache DIR` (`22.zip`, `47.zip`, `all.dat`, `defect.txt`). `--group station` restores only the rStation data. No different version is silently substituted. Unmodified original papers/third-party source code are not bundled as MIT works. The historical PDFs and the new manuscript contain attributed quotations of mathematical facts and may embed font subsets under the fonts' own licenses.
